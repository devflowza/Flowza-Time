# Walkthrough narration

Voice-over for `FlowZa-Time-Enterprise-Walkthrough.mp4` (3 min 45 s). Timestamps are where each scene starts.

## 0:00 · Introduction

> Welcome to FlowZa Time Enterprise: cloud attendance for multi-branch organisations across Oman and the GCC.
> Let's walk through it, from the first device to the live dashboard.

## 0:12 · 1. Configure devices

> Start with your devices. In Devices, choose Register device, and pick the provider: ZKTeco, Hikvision, Suprema, Anviz or Matrix.
> Give the terminal a code, a name, a branch and its timezone.
> Push terminals get their own URL and token, and come online with their first heartbeat. Cloud and server connections can be tested right away.

## 0:36 · 2. Branches and sites

> Next, your sites. Each branch has a code, a city, a timezone, weekly off days and a holiday calendar.
> The branch timezone decides the attendance date, so Muscat and Riyadh both stay correct.
> Add a geofence to check where web and mobile check-ins happen.

## 0:53 · 3. Employees, departments and shifts

> Now add your people: employee number, branch, department, designation and line manager. Or import hundreds at once from a CSV file.
> In Shifts and schedules, define fixed or flexible shifts, with breaks, grace periods and punch windows, including overnight shifts.
> Then assign shifts to a branch, a department, a team, or one employee. The most specific assignment wins.

## 1:17 · 4. Changing shifts

> To change a shift, assign the new one with an effective from date, and an end date if it's temporary.
> Change many employees at once with a bulk action, or let employees request a change from their own portal.
> Past days are recalculated automatically.

## 1:34 · 5. Working at another branch (branch deployment)

> Now, Salim works in Muscat, but is asked to cover the Sohar branch for two weeks.
> In Branch deployments, choose Deploy employee. Pick Sohar, the first and last day, and keep Add to the branch's terminals ticked.
> On day one, Salim is added to the Sohar terminals with ID and card, and removed automatically after the last day. A fingerprint is enrolled once on site.
> Salim's shift, holidays and payroll stay with Muscat, so the attendance record stays complete, without manual corrections.

## 2:06 · 6. Leave management

> Leave is built in. Set up annual, sick, emergency or maternity leave, with yearly days, monthly accrual and carry forward.
> Employees apply from their portal, and see their balance before they submit.
> Approved leave appears on the team calendar, and those days count as leave, not absence.

## 2:24 · 7. Line-manager approvals

> Every request goes to the right person. Each employee can have a line manager, and a secondary manager as backup.
> Leave, corrections, regularisations, shift changes and swaps all land in one Approvals inbox.
> Approve, reject, or ask for information. One by one, in bulk, or straight from an email.
> HR can add approval workflows of up to five levels, with escalation when nobody acts in time.

## 2:51 · 8. Shift swap request

> Need a shift swap? In My shift, choose Request a swap, then pick the day and a colleague.
> The line manager approves it in the inbox, and both schedules change for that day only.

## 3:03 · 9. Settings and dashboard

> Finally, configure and monitor. Settings cover regional options, attendance rules, sync, notifications and security, while roles decide who sees which branch.
> Under Settings, Dashboard, pick one of seven styles, and a layout: Overview, Operations, or Executive.
> The dashboard shows today at a glance: who is present, absent, on leave or late.
> Then the attendance trend, attendance by branch, recent activity, and the approvals waiting for you, with reports in PDF, Excel or CSV.

## 3:36 · Close

> FlowZa Time. People on time, business on track.
> See it for yourself at flowza.com.
